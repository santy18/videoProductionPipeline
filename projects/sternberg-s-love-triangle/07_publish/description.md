The psychology of love: every kind of love is a mix of just 3 ingredients 💛

Sternberg's triangular theory of love (1986) says love is built from intimacy (closeness), passion (the spark) and commitment (the decision to stay). Mix two and you get romantic love, companionate love or fatuous love. All three? Consummate love. A simple way to understand relationships and why some feel different from others.
