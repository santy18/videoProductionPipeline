ADHD explained: it's not a lack of attention, it's trouble steering it 🧠

What is ADHD, really? Many researchers describe adult and child ADHD as a problem of attention regulation, not missing attention. People with ADHD often report hyperfocus on things they love, yet struggle to start boring tasks. About 6% of US adults have ADHD (CDC, 2023), and it runs in families (74% heritability across twin studies).
